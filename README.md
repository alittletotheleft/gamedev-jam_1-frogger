# RETRO GAMEJAM
**Theme:** Retro Games with a Twist

**Inspiration:** Frogger but you play as the cars

**Title:** Freeway Frogs

## Freeway Frogs
Evil frogs are taking over the world! The only thing between you and them is the expressway. You must send down cars to crush every frog that dares cross the road.
Send various cars and earn community upgrades to help you along the way!

_Disclaimer: No frogs were harmed in the making of this game. All frogs represented are evil frogs from space that have come to destroy our planet and are deserving of being crushed._

## How to Play
1. Purchase a car from the sidebar
2. Hover your mouse over the side of the lane you want the car to start
3. Click to release the car on the freeway!
4. Hit the frogs the damage them
5. Hit other cars to create **explosions!** Explosions deal extra damage
6. Clear waves and get upgrades for clearing multiple waves.
